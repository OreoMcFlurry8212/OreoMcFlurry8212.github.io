01 / The person behind the pixels

Empathy-led. Creativity-fuelled. Technology-inspired.

“How can we make technology more intuitive, useful, and human?”

I'm Durva — an engineering graduate, digital designer, and curious problem-solver interested in how people experience technology.

My journey began with Electronics and Communication Engineering, where I explored the technical side of systems through programming and an ESP32-CAM image-stylization project. Along the way, I found myself drawn to the human side of technology: how an interface communicates, how a product feels to use, and how thoughtful design can make complex things simpler.

I've since worked on UI/UX, graphic design, and client websites, and gained professional experience as a Quality Reviewer at Ethara AI. I'm now pursuing an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, with an interest in software-oriented and interactive systems.

I'm building toward work that brings together design thinking, technical understanding, and responsible, useful AI experiences.
